Signing

The keystore never passes through this page. You create it yourself and, for GitHub, store it as a repository secret.

Back the keystore up in more than one place. If you publish an app and lose its key, you can never ship an update to it — there is no recovery, only a new listing that your users will not follow.

Before you plan on the Play Store

Google Play's minimum functionality policy rejects apps that only display a website. Bundled offline files, downloads, a toolbar and bottom navigation all argue that this is an app rather than a shortcut. For staff phones and internal distribution, none of that applies — sideload the APK and you are done.
