06Paid features

Sell premium. Mark buttons as premium and they stay locked behind a paywall screen until the user enters an unlock code you sold them — no store account, no backend, works offline. Your web page can gate its own features too via window.PackrApp.

The offer

Unlock codes

Codes are generated here and only their fingerprints are built into the app — the codes themselves exist nowhere but the file you download. One code unlocks one device; codes are reusable unless you rebuild with a new set.

Download codes.txt
Save the downloaded file — codes cannot be recovered from the app, only replaced by rebuilding. New codes add to the set; "Remove all" starts over.

Selling through the app stores?

Google Play and the Apple App Store require their own billing (Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase) for digital goods sold inside store-distributed apps — payment links and unlock codes there can get the app rejected. This system is built for the QR-install / sideload route, internal apps, and apps selling real-world goods or services. For store IAP you would extend the generated projects with the store SDKs.

07Signing

Debug builds carry a throwaway key — fine for your own phone, useless for stores. Release builds are signed with a keystore you own and must never lose.

Use a keystore

Back the keystore up in more than one place. Lose the key after publishing and you can never update the app again — there is no recovery.

08Build & install

APK installs directly on Android phones. AAB is the format the Google Play Console accepts. Build, then scan the QR code with the phone's camera to install.

09The app stores

Both stores take this app — with paperwork. Google Play wants a signed AAB from section 08. The Apple App Store cannot take an APK at all: Packr generates the matching Xcode project instead, and a Mac finishes the job.

Google Play

  1. Build Release + AAB in section 08, signed with your keystore.
  2. Create a developer account at play.google.com/console (US$25, once).
  3. Create the app, upload the .aab under Production → Create release.
  4. Fill the store listing, content rating and data-safety forms, then roll out.

Fair warning: Play's minimum-functionality policy rejects apps that are just a website in a shell. Bundled offline files argue your case; a wrapped public URL usually fails review. For staff phones, skip the store — the QR install above is enough.

Apple App Store

Packr generates a complete Xcode project of this same app — WKWebView shell, your files, icon, colours and permissions. Building and uploading it needs Xcode on a Mac and an Apple Developer membership (US$99/year). Full steps ship inside the download as APP-STORE-STEPS.md.
